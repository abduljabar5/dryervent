Booking & Scheduling

How do I book dryer vent cleaning?

Use the online booking form. Pick your service, tell us whether the vent exits through the roof or the wall, choose a date and two-hour arrival window, and enter your address. You get instant confirmation by email and text.

How soon can you come out?

Usually within 2–4 days, and often next day. Same-day slots open up when there are cancellations; call or text us if you need urgent service.

Do you offer weekend dryer vent cleaning?

Yes. Saturday appointments are available at the same price, and Sunday mornings on request.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes, free of charge with at least 12 hours notice. Reply to your confirmation text or email, or call us.

What if I don't know whether my vent is on the roof or the wall?

Pick "Not sure" during booking. You are booked at the $139 wall rate, and if we find a roof vent we confirm the $199 roof rate with you before starting. Our booking form also shows animated illustrations to help you identify it.

Will I get a reminder before my appointment?

Yes. You get a confirmation immediately, a reminder the evening before, and a text when the technician is on the way.

Do I need to prepare anything before the appointment?

Remove items on top of and in front of the dryer, make sure the laundry room path is clear, and let us know about gates or dogs. For roof vents, keep the ladder area clear of cars.

Can I book for a relative or a rental property?

Yes. Enter the service address and the on-site contact's name and phone in the booking form. We text the on-site contact when we are on the way and send the receipt to you.

Do you send the same technician each time?

We are a small local company, so in most cases yes.
