Minnesota Winters & Local Questions

Does Minnesota winter affect my dryer vent?

Yes. Cold caps condense moisture, which makes lint damp and sticky so it packs harder. Snow drifts block low wall vents, and ice can freeze flappers shut. Fall is the best time to clean before winter.

What is the best time of year to clean a dryer vent in Minnesota?

Fall, before heavy winter use and snow. Spring is a close second, especially for roof vents, to clear nests and winter lint buildup.

Do you clean dryer vents in winter?

Yes, year-round. For roof vents in icy conditions we clean from inside and return free to verify the cap when it is safe to walk the roof.

Is dryer vent cleaning required by code in Minnesota?

The Minnesota Mechanical Code sets installation requirements, like rigid metal duct, no screens, and a maximum developed length, but it does not mandate a cleaning schedule for single-family homes. Many condo associations and insurers do require annual cleaning.

Why is my dryer vent frosting over in winter?

Humid exhaust freezes on contact with a cold cap. Light frost is normal; thick ice that blocks the flapper means airflow is too slow to clear the moisture, which points to lint buildup.
