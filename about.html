A small company that does one thing well.

We clean dryer vents. Not air ducts, not carpets, not gutters. Just the one 4-inch duct that causes two-cycle dry times and 2,900 house fires a year.

Why we started

Every Twin Cities home has a dryer vent and almost none of them have ever been cleaned. The companies that do offer it usually bolt it onto air duct cleaning as a 20-minute add-on, or quote a teaser price and add trip fees on the invoice. We wanted a service that was straightforward: published flat rates, a technician who specializes in it, an airflow number that proves it worked, and a booking form that takes a minute.

How we work

  • Published prices. $139 wall vents, $199 roof vents. That's it.
  • Measured results. Airflow before and after, on your receipt.
  • No pressure. Add-ons are quoted on site and you can always say no.
  • Insured. Certificate of insurance available for associations and property managers.
  • Local. Based in Minneapolis, serving all seven metro counties with no trip charge.

Our guarantee

100% satisfaction or we come back free. If your dryer isn't drying noticeably faster after a cleaning, call us and we'll come back and find out why at no charge.
