Dryer vent questions from Rosemount

When do I pay?

After the job is done and you're satisfied. We accept credit and debit cards, cash, Zelle, and Venmo. No deposit is required to book.

What if you find a bird nest or extra work?

We show you the issue and the add-on price before doing anything extra. Bird nest removal is $69. You can always decline the add-on and we will complete the standard cleaning as booked.

Does homeowners insurance cover dryer vent cleaning?

Routine cleaning is maintenance and is not covered. However, some insurers ask about dryer vent maintenance after a fire claim, and documented annual cleaning can help. We email a receipt with before/after notes you can keep on file.

Can I get a quote without booking?

Yes, but you don't need one. Pricing is flat and listed on the pricing page. If you tell us whether the vent exits through the roof or the wall, you know your exact price.

Does Minnesota winter affect my dryer vent?

Yes. Cold caps condense moisture, which makes lint damp and sticky so it packs harder. Snow drifts block low wall vents, and ice can freeze flappers shut. Fall is the best time to clean before winter.

Do you clean dryer vents in winter?

Yes, year-round. For roof vents in icy conditions we clean from inside and return free to verify the cap when it is safe to walk the roof.

Is dryer vent cleaning required by code in Minnesota?

The Minnesota Mechanical Code sets installation requirements, like rigid metal duct, no screens, and a maximum developed length, but it does not mandate a cleaning schedule for single-family homes. Many condo associations and insurers do require annual cleaning.

Do you serve Minneapolis and Saint Paul?

Yes, every neighborhood in both cities, plus the surrounding suburbs. There is no trip charge anywhere in the metro.
