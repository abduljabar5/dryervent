Quick answers

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost in the Twin Cities?

Our flat rate is $139 for a wall or ground-level vent and $199 for a roof vent, with no trip charge or hidden fees. Most Twin Cities companies charge $100–$250 depending on vent location and add trip fees; we publish our pricing so you know the total before you book.

Why does a roof dryer vent cost more to clean?

Roof vents require a ladder setup, roof safety gear, clearing the cap from above, and cleaning the vertical run from both ends. It takes longer and carries more risk, so the flat rate is $199 instead of $139.

How do I book dryer vent cleaning?

Use the online booking form. Pick your service, tell us whether the vent exits through the roof or the wall, choose a date and two-hour arrival window, and enter your address. You get an instant confirmation by email.

How soon can you come out?

Usually within 2–4 days, and often next day. Same-day slots open up when there are cancellations; call or text us if you need urgent service.

What is dryer vent cleaning?

Dryer vent cleaning is the removal of lint and debris from the entire duct that runs from the back of your dryer to the vent cap outside your home. We use a rotating brush system and a high-powered vacuum to scrub the full length of the duct, clear the exterior cap, and verify airflow afterward. It is different from cleaning the lint screen, which only catches a portion of the lint your dryer produces.

How do I know if my dryer vents through the roof?

Look outside for a vent cap near your dryer's side of the house. If there is no 4-inch cap on any exterior wall and your laundry is on an upper floor or an interior wall, look at the roof for a small hooded cap, usually gray or black. During booking we show animated pictures of both so you can pick.
