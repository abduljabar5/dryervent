Pricing & Payment

How much does dryer vent cleaning cost in the Twin Cities?

Our flat rate is $139 for a wall or ground-level vent and $199 for a roof vent, with no trip charge or hidden fees. Most Twin Cities companies charge $100–$250 depending on vent location and add trip fees; we publish our pricing so you know the total before you book.

Why does a roof dryer vent cost more to clean?

Roof vents require a ladder setup, roof safety gear, clearing the cap from above, and cleaning the vertical run from both ends. It takes longer and carries more risk, so the flat rate is $199 instead of $139.

Are there any hidden fees or trip charges?

No. The price you see when you book is the price you pay unless you add optional services on site, which we confirm with you first. No trip charge, no fuel surcharge, no weekend upcharge.

When do I pay?

After the job is done and you're satisfied. We accept credit and debit cards, cash, Zelle, and Venmo. No deposit is required to book.

Do you offer discounts for multiple dryers or units?

Yes. A second dryer at the same address is $89, and multi-unit buildings start at $79 per unit for scheduled whole-building cleanings. Contact us for a quote on 4 or more units.

What if you find a bird nest or extra work?

We show you the issue and the add-on price before doing anything extra. Bird nest removal is $69. You can always decline the add-on and we will complete the standard cleaning as booked.

Is dryer vent cleaning worth the cost?

A clogged vent adds 15–30 minutes to each load, which for a family doing 8 loads a week is roughly $80–$120 a year in extra electricity or gas, plus a shorter dryer lifespan. One cleaning typically pays for itself within the year before you count fire prevention.

Does homeowners insurance cover dryer vent cleaning?

Routine cleaning is maintenance and is not covered. However, some insurers ask about dryer vent maintenance after a fire claim, and documented annual cleaning can help. We email a receipt with before/after notes you can keep on file.

Do you charge more for long duct runs?

No. Our flat rate covers duct runs up to 35 feet, which includes nearly every home in the metro. If a run is unusually long or has more than four elbows we tell you before starting, and the price stays within the posted range.

Can I get a quote without booking?

Yes, but you don't need one. Pricing is flat and listed on the pricing page. If you tell us whether the vent exits through the roof or the wall, you know your exact price.

Do you price match other dryer vent cleaning companies?

If a licensed, insured local competitor offers a lower written price for the same service, send it over and we will match or beat it.
