Apartments, Condos & Townhomes

Do you clean dryer vents in apartments and condos?

Yes. We serve individual units and whole buildings. Condo and apartment vents are often long runs to the roof or a shared chase, and we coordinate with the association or property manager for roof access.

Who is responsible for dryer vent cleaning in a condo?

Usually the unit owner is responsible for the duct from the dryer to the exterior wall of the unit, and the association for common areas and roof caps. Check your declarations; we can clean both and bill separately.

Do you offer pricing for property managers?

Yes. Whole-building scheduled cleanings start at $79 per unit with a single invoice, certificate of insurance, and a unit-by-unit report for your records.

Do you clean townhome dryer vents?

Yes. Twin Cities townhomes often vent through the roof or soffit with very long runs. Many associations schedule us to do every unit in a building on the same day.

Should landlords clean dryer vents between tenants?

Yes. Turnover is the easiest time to clean and document the vent, and it reduces fire liability and tenant complaints about dry times.
