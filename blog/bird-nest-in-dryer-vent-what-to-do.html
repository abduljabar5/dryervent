There's a Bird Nest in My Dryer Vent. Now What?

Every April the calls start: "I hear chirping in the wall by the dryer." A dryer vent is a warm, sheltered 4-inch tunnel, which is exactly what a starling is looking for.

How to confirm it's a nest

  • Chirping or scratching behind the dryer or in the ceiling above it
  • Grass, twigs or feathers around the exterior cap
  • The flapper propped partly open when the dryer is off
  • Dry times that got long suddenly, in spring
  • Small insects or mites in the laundry room

Why it's urgent

Dry grass and twigs packed against lint, inches from a heating element, is kindling. Nest material also blocks airflow completely rather than partially, so the dryer overheats fast. Stop using the dryer until the vent is cleared.

What the law says

European starlings and house sparrows, the two most common vent nesters, are invasive species not protected by the Migratory Bird Treaty Act. They can be removed anytime. Native birds like chickadees and wrens are protected while there are eggs or chicks in the nest. We identify the species when we open the cap and explain your options. Usually it's starlings and we remove it on the spot.

How we remove it

  1. Open the cap and extract nest material by hand and with a retrieval tool.
  2. Rotary-brush and vacuum the full duct; nests are often paired with heavy lint.
  3. Sanitize the duct.
  4. Install a dryer-rated pest-guard cap so it doesn't happen again.

Keeping them out

Never put window screen or hardware cloth over a dryer vent. It traps lint and is against code. A proper pest-guard cap uses a hood with louvers or a wide cage that lets lint pass and blocks birds and rodents. We install them for $89 including the cap, or read more about nest removal.

Related questions

How do I know if there is a bird nest in my dryer vent?

Chirping from the wall or ceiling near the dryer, twigs or grass around the exterior cap, a flapper stuck open, or a sudden drop in airflow in spring. Starlings and sparrows love the warm 4-inch opening.

Can you remove a bird nest from a dryer vent?

Yes. Bird nest removal is a $69 add-on. We remove the nest material, clean the duct, sanitize, and recommend a pest-proof cap so it doesn't happen again.

What if there are live birds or eggs in the vent?

Minnesota law protects most native birds and active nests during nesting season. Starlings and house sparrows are not protected and can be removed anytime. For protected species with eggs or chicks we may have to wait a few weeks; we will tell you what we find and your options.

How do I keep birds out of my dryer vent?

Install a dryer-rated pest-guard cap with a cage or louvers wide enough to let lint pass but small enough to block birds. Never use window screen. We install these for $89 including the cap.

Can mice or squirrels get into a dryer vent?

Yes, especially through broken flappers on ground-level vents and roof caps near trees. Signs include droppings behind the dryer and chewed flex hose. A pest-guard cap stops them.

Is bird nest material in a dryer vent dangerous?

Very. Dry grass and twigs packed against hot lint are kindling, and nest material also blocks airflow completely. Bird nests are one of the most common causes of the dryer fires we hear about.
