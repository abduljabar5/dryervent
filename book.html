Where does your dryer vent exit the house?

This is the only thing that changes the price. Watch the lint travel and pick the one that matches your home.

How do I find my vent?

Walk around the outside of the house on the laundry-room side and look for a 4-inch hooded cap with a flap. Run the dryer: the flap should move and you'll feel warm air. If there is no cap on any wall and your laundry is upstairs, look at the roof for a small gray or black hood about the size of a coffee can. Full guide with pictures →
